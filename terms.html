Using Tuck-a-Boo

Tuck-a-Boo is a bedtime-story app for families. You may use it for personal, non-commercial use with your own children. Please be kind and use it as it was meant to be used.

Stories written by AI

Stories are written by an AI model running on your phone. We design it to be gentle and to avoid scary or unsuitable content, but an AI can make mistakes. Please read a story first if you want to be sure, and stay with your child. You are responsible for choosing what your child hears.

The ready-made collection

The bedtime collection is written for Tuck-a-Boo. You may enjoy it in the app, but you may not copy or resell it.

Your voice

Only record your own voice, or the voice of someone who has agreed. Please do not use the voice feature to imitate another person without their permission. Your voice stays on your phone.

Tuck-a-Boo Plus

Tuck-a-Boo is free to download. The heroes and buddies, My voice and two stories are free. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy; if a purchase is refunded, Plus locks again.
