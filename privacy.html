Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

Tuck-a-Boo keeps your family's information on your phone and nowhere else. We do not have servers that receive your child's name, your stories or your voice. We cannot see them, sell them or share them, because we never get them.

What stays on your phone

Your child's name, age and pronouns; the hero and buddy you design; every story; your settings; your voice recordings and the voice made from them. All of it is stored in the app's private storage on your device. Stories are written on your phone by the storyteller model, and your voice is made on your phone too.

What we do not do

We have no accounts or sign-in. We show no ads. We use no analytics, no tracking and no advertising identifiers. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or anything else from your phone. We do not upload your recordings.

The only internet use

Three things can use the internet. First, downloads: with Tuck-a-Boo Plus the app downloads the storyteller model once, and (if you choose) the voice model and the natural voice. These come from public file servers (our model library on Hugging Face and the original publishers' servers). Like any download, those servers can see your phone's internet address, but nothing about your child or your family is sent. Second, Google Play: the app asks it for the price of Tuck-a-Boo Plus and whether your Google account owns it (see Purchases). Third, only if a grown-up chooses it: "Report this story" opens a short Google Form in your browser, with the story's title and page filled in, and nothing is sent unless you press Send there. After the downloads, stories, voices and reading work without internet.

Purchases

Tuck-a-Boo Plus is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment under its own terms and privacy policy; we never see your name, card or payment details. The app only learns from Google Play whether this Google account owns Plus, so it can unlock it, also on a new phone or after reinstalling.

The phone's own voice

If you use the phone's built-in "system voice" for reading, that voice is provided by your phone's speech engine (for example Google Text-to-speech) and follows your phone's own settings and privacy policy. The "My voice" and "Natural voice" options run entirely inside Tuck-a-Boo.

Children

Tuck-a-Boo is designed for parents to use with their children. It collects no personal information from children or from parents.

Deleting your data

Delete a story in the Library by pressing and holding it. Delete a child profile in the Heroes tab. Remove your voice in Settings → My voice. Uninstalling the app erases everything.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear here, with a new date. Because we do not collect your data, any change will only make it clearer, never weaker.
